# JOAN Company Value Capture v0

## Status

This is a business architecture and governance proposal, not a public license,
price commitment, payment offer, trademark registration, or legal conclusion.
The repository remains all-rights-reserved until LED ACTION LLC adopts written
terms after qualified legal and tax review.

## Objective

JOAN should be inexpensive to adopt and useful without an LED ACTION LLC
backend while creating durable reasons to pay LED ACTION LLC when commercial
use becomes valuable. Copyright alone cannot guarantee revenue, and a mandatory
protocol toll can be removed by an independent implementation. The defensible
asset is a combined system of brand trust, official conformance, release
provenance, enterprise intellectual property, and customer relationships.

## Free adoption layer

Subject to the future license decision, the following should be implementable
without a protocol fee:

- the stable JOAN Lattice wire specification;
- canonical conformance vectors and offline verification;
- local compilation and bounded deterministic execution;
- peer-to-peer discovery, transport, and direct service contracts;
- independent implementations that do not claim official certification.

This layer is the distribution engine. Artificially disabling it would make
GitHub discovery and independent operation unattractive.

## LED ACTION LLC revenue products

### 1. JOAN Certified conformance

Vendors pay for a time-bounded, version-specific conformance assessment and the
right to display an authorized certification mark under written rules. The
certificate binds product identity, test-root digest, supported profile,
expiry, and revocation status. Buying a certificate cannot change test results
or raise a service's reputation score.

### 2. Enterprise Runtime and policy packs

Keep separately authored enterprise modules proprietary or commercially
licensed: hardware-backed key custody, regulated-industry policy, fleet
administration, audit export, data-retention controls, high-availability
coordination, and long-term support. The open interoperability layer must not
silently import these modules.

### 3. OEM and embedded licensing

Charge organizations that want an official JOAN distribution embedded under
their product, extended support lifecycle, private compatibility profiles, or
the right to use specified JOAN marks in product names. Generic protocol
compatibility and official brand endorsement must remain distinct.

### 4. Verification and security services

Sell reproducible-build review, provider security assessment, dispute-policy
validation, adversarial simulation, migration certification, and signed release
provenance. Independent auditors can perform technical work; only LED ACTION
LLC can issue an LED-owned official mark.

### 5. Optional official market

An official commercial directory may charge a disclosed success fee only when
a buyer and provider settle a paid contract through that market. Discovery and
direct peer contracts remain possible without the market. The fee belongs to
the market service, not the JOAN protocol, compiler, VM, or Proof of Useful
Service object.

### 6. Support and release lifecycle

Offer annual support, incident response, compatibility maintenance, migration
assistance, and long-term release branches. These contracts can finance any
future hosted infrastructure before it is provisioned.

## Zero-owner-hosting collection path

The initial revenue path does not require an always-on LED server:

1. A customer orders a certificate, license, or support package through a
   third-party commerce provider or signed enterprise agreement.
2. Payment goes directly to LED ACTION LLC through that provider.
3. LED ACTION LLC evaluates a submitted evidence bundle offline or in a
   customer-funded CI environment.
4. An offline corporate root signs a bounded certificate or license manifest.
5. The signed artifact is delivered through GitHub Releases and independent
   mirrors; peer nodes verify it locally.
6. Public revocation snapshots are short-lived, signed, mirrored, and cached so
   GitHub or LED downtime does not stop the data plane.

Third-party processors, legal work, taxes, certificate review, support labor,
and signing custody are not free. This design removes mandatory fixed backend
hosting at launch; it does not promise zero business expense.

## Recommended licensing decision

Do not publish the current all-rights-reserved repository as if it were an open
project: recipients currently have no general permission to use or distribute
it. Before public alpha, counsel should select one explicit structure:

1. **Adoption-first open core:** a standard license for protocol, compiler, and
   SDK, with proprietary enterprise modules and controlled trademarks.
2. **File-level reciprocal core:** MPL-2.0 for selected files, allowing larger
   proprietary products while requiring distributed modifications to covered
   files to remain available under MPL terms.
3. **Dual commercial structure:** a reciprocal license plus a separate paid
   commercial license where the reciprocal obligations are unacceptable.

The license choice must account for every contributor. A CLA, copyright
assignment, or other counsel-approved contribution policy is required before
accepting code that LED ACTION LLC expects to license commercially.

## Brand and key control

- LED ACTION LLC owns and controls any JOAN product trademark applications,
  certification-mark program, domains, and official signing identities.
- Release, certification, and emergency-revocation keys are separate.
- Corporate recovery requires documented multi-person or professional custody;
  no single Mac is the permanent root of the business.
- A certification mark must have objective public rules and cannot be used as a
  disguised pay-to-rank mechanism.
- Forks may describe compatibility truthfully but cannot imply LED endorsement
  or use controlled marks outside the published policy.

## Economic invariants

- no speculative JOAN token, mining reward, gas auction, or forced wallet;
- no fee on local compilation, verification, or direct peer communication;
- every commercial fee is quoted before acceptance and included in total cost;
- provider payment, processor charge, tax, and LED fee are separate receipt
  fields;
- no revenue share is deducted unless the payer approved its exact policy;
- failed or rejected work cannot create a success fee;
- certification payment never changes conformance truth;
- real-money adapters remain outside the language VM and require jurisdictional
  review.

## Adoption-to-revenue gates

| Gate | Evidence | Commercial action |
|---|---|---|
| A0 | 3/3 external GitHub trials complete | no charge; remove onboarding friction |
| A1 | at least 2/3 repeat or recommend | publish license and compatibility policy |
| A2 | 10 independent active projects | interview for one paid certification problem |
| A3 | 3 signed letters of intent | define certificate rules and price experiment |
| A4 | first prepaid customer | fund only the infrastructure that contract needs |
| A5 | repeat renewal and positive margin | expand support and certified profiles |

If no user repeats after ten qualified trials, narrow or change the product
before adding a marketplace, payment rail, or hosted control plane.

## Required metrics

- unique verified installations and release/update success;
- external trial completion, time-to-first-result, repeat intent, and referrals;
- conformance submissions, pass rate, review labor, and renewal rate;
- qualified leads, paid conversion, annual contract value, gross margin, churn,
  support load, processor cost, refunds, and disputes;
- direct peer contracts versus official-market contracts;
- concentration risk by customer, provider, processor, jurisdiction, and key.

No hidden telemetry is added to the language. Metrics require explicit opt-in,
public GitHub aggregates, or contractual enterprise reporting.

## Legal and operational gates

Before public use or revenue collection:

- approve the public-source/commercial license and contribution agreement;
- run JOAN name clearance and trademark strategy;
- define objective conformance and certification-mark rules;
- secure GitHub, domain, payment, tax, and signing-key recovery;
- publish terms, privacy, security reporting, refund, support, and sanctions
  handling appropriate to the offered service;
- obtain review for money transmission, marketplace, tax, employment,
  export-control, privacy, and automated-dispute exposure where applicable.
