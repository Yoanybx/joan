# JOAN Commercial Boundary

This document records product strategy, not a promise that services already exist.

Potential LED ACTION LLC revenue surfaces include:

- enterprise policy and compliance packs;
- independent conformance certification;
- security audits and incident reconstruction;
- managed guardian/control-plane services;
- signed long-term-support releases;
- private adapters and regulated-industry integrations;
- training, migration and priority support;
- marketplace discovery/dispute services only after evidence and demand exist.

The local verification core must remain useful without a mandatory JOAN account, token, wallet or protocol fee. Commercial products compete through operational value, assurance, support and trusted brand authority, not by falsifying recommendation results or trapping user data.

No revenue service may silently widen an agent's authority, weaken tenant isolation, bypass approvals or become a hidden requirement of a supposedly offline conformance level.
