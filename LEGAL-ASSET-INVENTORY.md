## Exact technical attachment

An inventory attachment is complete only when it identifies one immutable Git
commit and includes the matching `.joan/evidence/latest.json`, source-tree
digest, `Cargo.lock`, release metadata, checksums, and generated `SBOM/`
directory. A moving branch name, local path, archive filename, or document title
is not an exact asset identity.

The attachment must be retained with the signed private instrument and company
acceptance record. Repository evidence can bind bytes and execution results; it
cannot prove legal ownership, human authorship of every expression, trademark
clearance, or the validity of a signature.
