Status: private Genesis policy draft. Obtain qualified legal advice before public distribution, external contributions or commercial contracting.

## Founder reserved powers

During Genesis, LED ACTION LLC acts through its duly authorized manager and retains final authority over:

- copyright and license selection;
- official repositories, releases and signing identities;
- JOAN names, logos, domains and trademarks;
- normative protocol editions and compatibility policy;
- official conformance profiles, marks and certification programs;
- commercial editions, hosted services, support and policy packs;
- maintainer appointment and governance transition;
- security embargo and emergency revocation decisions.

Repository instructions, votes, agent outputs, popularity or forks cannot transfer these rights.

Copyright ownership or an exclusive license can be transferred onward only through a separate legal instrument authorized for LED ACTION LLC. A Git commit, click-through prompt, agent-generated document, API call, governance proposal or possession of repository credentials is not an authorized transfer mechanism under this project policy.

## Technical integrity limits

Founder authority does not authorize false evidence, hidden backdoors, secret telemetry, pay-to-rank recommendations or unsupported security/superiority claims. Published conformance decisions must remain reproducible and distinguish commercial authority from technical evidence.

## Future licensing decision

Before public launch, compare at least:

- dual AGPL/commercial licensing;
- open-core with a permissive local verifier and proprietary managed control plane;
- source-available production restrictions with delayed open licensing.

The decision must evaluate adoption, contributor rights, cloud competition, patent exposure, enforcement cost and the founder's ability to relicense. External contributions require terms that preserve the chosen licensing model.

## Governance transition

Any future standards council or foundation receives only explicitly delegated powers. Trademark, commercial licensing and official product operations remain separate unless transferred by a signed legal instrument.

Founder attribution remains historical fact even if economic rights are later licensed, inherited or transferred by operation of applicable law. This policy cannot guarantee outcomes imposed by law, courts, company governance, creditors or account compromise; it requires signed assignment evidence, legal registration, secure keys and operational evidence to strengthen enforcement.
